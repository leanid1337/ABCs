    FAQ

    Rights, files, payment and timing.

    5 questions

    Is a premade really exclusive?

    Yes. One design, one owner. Once it sells it leaves the collection and is never sold again. You get full commercial rights to print and sell it under your label.

    What files do I get?

    4000x4000px at 300 DPI: transparent PNG, JPG and a layered PSD, plus a mockup preview. Ready for DTG, DTF or screen print.

    How do I pay?

    Payment details are shared once your order is confirmed. Premades are paid in full before files are sent; commissions are split into a deposit and a final payment.

    How fast is turnaround?

    Premades: files the same day payment clears. Commissions: 3-5 days for a single, 7-10 for a capsule, 14-21 for a collection.

    How many revisions?

    2 on a single, 3 per design on capsules and collections. Premades can get a color swap to match your garment.
