    5 questions

    Is a premade really exclusive?

    Yes. One design, one owner. Once you buy it, it comes off the shop and is never sold again. You get full commercial rights to print and sell it under your brand.

    What files do I get?

    4000x4000px at 300 DPI: transparent PNG, JPG and a layered PSD, plus a mockup preview. Ready for DTG, DTF or screen print.

    How do I pay?

    PayPal or Remitly. Premades are paid in full before files are sent. Commissions are 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.

    How fast is turnaround?

    Premades: files the same day payment clears. Commissions: 3-5 days for a single graphic, 7-10 for a capsule, 14-21 for a full drop.

    How many revisions?

    2 on a single graphic, 3 per design on capsules and full drops. Premades can get a color swap to match your garment.
